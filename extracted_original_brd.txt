1. Project Overview
Nitro Racing is a bike service center business with multiple branches (for example Ambattur and Avadi). This
project delivers a web application with a public website and a secure management portal. The portal handles
branches, staff, bike service job cards, status tracking, invoicing and sales dashboards.
Goals
  Manage all branches and staff from one place
 Replace paper records with digital job cards for every bike that comes in
 Generate PDF invoices quickly
 Give owners clear branch-wise and overall business insights
 Work well on mobile, since staff will mostly use phones
2. Scope
In scope
  Public website (see section 3)
 Login-based management portal: store management, user management, job cards, status tracking,
invoices and dashboards
3. Public Website
Pages: Home, About Us, Services, Enquiry / Contact, Testimonials, Login.
  Enquiry form submissions are saved in the system and emailed to the business.
 Login button in the header. No Register button.

6. Non-Functional Requirements
  Responsive: works on mobile, laptop and desktop, with a mobile-first design for the job card and invoice
screens.
 Scalable: supports many branches, users and records without redesign.
 Security: encrypted passwords, HTTPS, role-based access on every page and API, data separated by
branch.
 Performance: pages load in about 3 seconds on a normal mobile connection.
 Availability and backup: daily automatic data backup.
 Browsers: latest Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox.

7. Assumptions
  One Super Admin account for the whole business.
 Staff will have internet access at the service center.
 Invoice format and branding will be provided by the client.
 English UI only (to be confirmed).
8. Open Questions for the Client
 1. How should profit be calculated? We need to know whether parts purchase cost and expenses will be
entered in the system.
2. Is GST/tax required on invoices? Please share the GST number and invoice format.
3. Should customers receive SMS/WhatsApp updates on status changes?
4. Should the Delivered status be added after Completed?
5. Can an Employee edit or delete a job card after creation, or only create and update status?
6. Does the client already have a domain, hosting and logo/brand colours?
7. Is old data (existing customers/records) to be migrated?
8. Is a language other than English (e.g. Tamil) needed?
